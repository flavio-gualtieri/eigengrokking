## Reading this

This reuses the seed=0 headline definition unfiltered -- flagged (train_acc<0.9) checkpoints are not dropped from the onset/last-checkpoint lookup, only annotated in the table above. A seed whose onset or last-window checkpoint itself is flagged should be read with that in mind before trusting its individual collapse factor.

The onset here is the 2x-chance crossing, a secondary definition (eigengrok/analysis/milestones.py): it is noise-dominated across seeds, and some of the seeds above cross it within the first few hundred steps, long before their primary onset (first test accuracy >= 0.5, the column next to it). A collapse factor measured from such an early step is not a post-onset collapse.
