# Sweep summary: grokking milestones, p=91

Generated by `python scripts/sweep_summary.py` from `reports/wd_sweep_accuracy_curves.csv`: 240 runs (2 init scales x 12 weight decays x 10 seeds), test accuracy logged every 100 steps to step 149,900.

Definitions (`eigengrok/analysis/milestones.py`; steps are on the 100-step log grid):

- `t_mem`: first step with train accuracy >= 0.99
- `t_onset` (primary): first step with test accuracy >= 0.5
- `t_grok`: first step with test accuracy >= 0.95, held for 5 consecutive evaluations
- `gap` = `t_onset` - `t_mem`
- collapse: test (or train) accuracy falling from >= 0.95 to < 0.5; the curve must climb back to >= 0.95 before the next one counts
- secondary onsets (sensitivity only): `onset_2x_chance` (first test accuracy > 2/p) and `onset_logistic` (exp(mu - 2 sigma) of a logistic fit in log-step, initialized at the 2x-chance crossing)

Intervals are 95% percentile bootstraps over seeds (10,000 resamples, seed 0). Pooled-over-weight-decay intervals resample seeds within each weight decay. Per-run values: `sweep_milestones.csv`; every column below with its interval: `sweep_summary.csv`.
